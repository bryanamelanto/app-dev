## Favorite K-Drama: While You Were Sleeping

*Genre:* Fantasy • Romance • Legal Drama  
*Released:* 2017  
*Main Cast:*
Bae Suzy, Lee Jong-suk, Jung Hae-in

---

## Overview
In While You Were Sleeping, a young woman named *Nam Hong-joo* has mysterious dreams that show future accidents and deaths.  
Her life changes when she meets *Jung Jae-chan*, a rookie prosecutor, and *Han Woo-tak*, a kind police officer.  
Together, they try to *change fate* and prevent the tragedies Hong-joo dreams of.  

---

## What Makes It Special
- Blends *romance*, *mystery*, and *supernatural elements* beautifully.  
- Shows how *destiny and free will* can shape lives.  
- The chemistry between the lead characters feels real and emotional.  
- Each episode leaves a meaningful lesson about hope and courage.  

---

## Memorable Quote
“Even if you can see the future, you can’t change it alone.” – *Jung Jae-chan*
